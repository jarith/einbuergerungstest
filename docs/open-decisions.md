# Open decisions

## Position shown in group study

**Question:** In Constitution and rights (27 questions), the first two source questions
are #1 and #3. After choosing the group and pressing Next, what position should the study
screen show? Decide later is available.

**Information needed:** Choose whether the position is relative to the selected group
(question 2 of 27) or to the full question bank (question 3 of 310).

**Blocks:** The group-study position requirement and its BDD scenario.

## Reopening a finished group

**Question:** A learner answers the final question in a group and finishes it. On a later
visit to that same group, where should study reopen? Decide later is available.

**Information needed:** Choose whether a new pass starts at the group's first question or
reopens its final question.

**Blocks:** The completed-group return requirement and its BDD scenario.

## Returning to all-question study

**Question:** A learner reaches source question #2 in all-question Study, switches to
Constitution and rights, reaches its second question (#3), then returns to all-question
Study. What should resume show? Decide later is available.

**Information needed:** Choose whether all-question Study keeps its own position at #2 or
follows the group's position at #3.

**Blocks:** The mode-switching position requirement and its BDD scenario.
