# 19 — Installation page

## Goal

Explain iPhone and iPad Home Screen installation and safe progress transfer.

## Work

- In pages/installation, add /settings/installation with a loader for offline readiness
  and relevant platform state; its render mounts InstallationPage with loading/error
  handling.
- Show the designed Safari steps: open in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen,
  then confirm Add. Explain that the installed app has separate storage from Safari and
  requires its own online offline-preparation step.
- Link to the backup controls so a learner can export in Safari and import into the
  installed app. Keep steps readable and touch friendly on iPhone and iPad.

## Complete when

The guide can be reached from Settings and gives accurate transfer and offline-readiness
guidance in both UI languages.
